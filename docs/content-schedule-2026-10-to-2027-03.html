Content Schedule Proposal · Prepared for Teeko

Getting the tourist from KLIA2 arrivals to a booked ride

A 6-month, three-language content programme for teeko.ai, built on a competitor gap analysis of the KLIA2 transport and Malaysian food SERPs. Every piece ends with a route to book at ttklia.com.
Client: Teeko (GM Ai Tours Sdn Bhd.) Market: Inbound travel, KLIA2 and onward Term: Oct 2026 to Mar 2027 Output: 190 pieces · EN / BM / 中文
The Situation
The Problem DA 3

teeko.ai earns about 44 organic visits a month from 163 ranking keywords, and none of them sit on page 1. The site has no link to ttklia.com, the booking destination the client named on 19 Sept 2026. Transport head terms are held by marketplaces with DA 37 to 46 and tens of thousands of referring domains. teeko.ai cannot win those in six months.

The Opportunity 60,000+

Monthly Malaysian searches sit on destination food terms, 10 of the 13 measured at difficulty 24 or lower: kl sentral food (8,100), klia2 food (8,100), ipoh food (8,100), penang food (9,900), bukit bintang food (5,400). Page 1 includes blogs as weak as DA 11. Teeko already has rated restaurant data for these cities, and ttklia.com sells the bus to most of them.

None of the ranking pages we benchmarked connects the ride to the meal. klia2.info explains the terminal but can't book anything. redBus and BusOnlineTicket sell tickets and say nothing about the place you are going. Food bloggers describe Ipoh and Melaka and never mention how to get there from the airport. Teeko is the only one that can put the arrival card, the SIM, the ride and the first meal on one page, because one group runs all of them. The programme is built around that gap: food and arrival content that ranks on soft ground, with every piece ending in a booking route at ttklia.com.

Four Pillars, One Destination

Every piece belongs to one pillar and every pillar ends at ttklia.com. Transport pieces convert directly. Food and destination pieces bring in far more traffic at lower difficulty, then hand the reader to the transport piece or straight to the booking page. The food pillar carries the volume; the transport pillar is where people book.

T · Arrival & TransportBook

KLIA2 routes, taxi, transit, MDAC. Small volumes, strong intent. Links to a specific ttklia.com route.

F · Food BridgeRank

Food guides for places ttklia.com runs buses to. Each carries a "Getting here from KLIA2" block.

S · ConnectivityCollect

SIM and eSIM decisions before landing. Routes to the KLIA2 SIM booking, then transport.

D · Destinations & SeasonalPlan

Things to do, festivals, Raya travel. Timed a month ahead of each search peak.

A new hub with no settled guide

Page 1 is launch news (The Straits Times, TheSmartLocal), marketplaces (BusOnlineTicket's terminal page, redBus SG), one coach operator's guide, Reddit and a YouTube vlog. SERP read · private driver KL and Genting

Held by OTAs and small operators

Klook, GetYourGuide, TripAdvisor and Daytrip list private transfers and day tours, beside small chauffeur firms and a Facebook group. One operator blog lists ten day trips from KL. None of them suggests where to eat on the way. Teeko's restaurant data can add meal stops along the route, ending at /car-charter.

Measured 3 October: the strongest term is one Malaysian data missed. "singapore to kl bus" gets 6,600 searches a month in Singapore at SD 21, against 590 in Malaysia, and the hub name "lalaport bbcc" alone gets 5,400 in Malaysia. Four first-choice phrasings measured zero and were retargeted: the KL to KLIA piece now targets "klia transfer" (1,300, SD 28), the hotel transfer piece "kl airport transfer" (50 MY, 70 SG), the Lalaport walking piece "things to do in bukit bintang" (880, SD 18), and the Genting driver piece "genting day trip" (50). Private-driver search demand is small (70/mo), so those two pieces are there to convert readers who are already on the site, not to bring in traffic. "klia2 to lalaport" measures 10 and is kept only because ttklia.com sells the route. The Singapore → Lalaport piece is written for that 6,600 demand, but its booking links are the return leg (Lalaport → Singapore) and the onward ride from Lalaport.

Decisions
1
Build: the food-bridge template. Every food guide ends with "Getting here from KLIA2" and a cta block to the matching ttklia.com route. This is the row no competitor has.
2
Build: one comparison per route. Bus, train, taxi and Grab on one page, the ride booked through ttklia.com, and no fares printed that we can't keep current.
3
Skip: head-term transport pages ("klia2 bus", "bus to klia"). The marketplaces hold them with over 1,000 times our referring domains. Go after the long tail and the food terms instead.
4
Skip: duplicating ttklia.com's own route guides. teeko.ai adds the traveller context (food, SIM, arrival card) and links to ttklia's route page.
5
Compensate for no photos: ask for transport-hub photos in Month 1. Until they arrive, write wayfinding from ttklia.com's own instructions and say where each step is.
6
Compensate for DA 3: target SD ≤ 24, build internal links from every food piece to its route piece, and add a cta link to ttklia.com on the 12 existing posts.
7
Ask: Simplified or Traditional Chinese, the reviewers, the reverse route, and restaurant rows for Genting, Melaka and Ipoh.
8
Never in published copy: competitor names, comparisons with named rivals, or claims about which provider is cheapest or best. This benchmark shapes the pages. It does not appear on them.
